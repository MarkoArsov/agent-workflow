# SettingRow

A setting as a row: what it is and what it changes on the left, the control on the right. Rows sit in a `pl-group` with hairline dividers, one group per topic.

**Provide** a short name, a one-line description in the person's terms, and one control: `pl-switch`, `pl-input pl-input--num`, `pl-select`, or `pl-radio` rows for a choice of three. Errors replace the description in `pl-help--error` and say the fix ("Use 1 minute or more").
